HERBAL TEA

5 Herbal Teas You Can Enjoy

Herbal teas can be a simple way to enjoy plant-based ingredients as part of a balanced lifestyle. They are available in many varieties, flavours and traditional preparations.

1. Peppermint Tea

Peppermint tea is traditionally enjoyed as a refreshing beverage. Many people enjoy it after meals or as part of their daily beverage routine.

2. Ginger Tea

Ginger tea is a warming beverage with a distinctive flavour. It can be enjoyed hot and is commonly prepared using fresh or dried ginger.

3. Chamomile Tea

Chamomile tea is often enjoyed as an evening drink and is commonly included in relaxation routines.

4. Hibiscus Tea

Hibiscus tea is a colourful and refreshing drink that can be enjoyed either hot or cold.

5. Green Tea

Green tea is widely enjoyed around the world as an everyday beverage. Unlike many herbal teas, green tea naturally contains caffeine.

Important Wellness Notice:

Herbal products are not suitable for everyone. Some may interact with medicines or may not be appropriate for people with certain health conditions.

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medicines, managing a health condition, or considering herbal products for a specific health purpose, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.
