5 Herbal Teas You Can Enjoy

Herbal teas can be a simple way to enjoy plant-based ingredients as part of a balanced lifestyle.

1. Peppermint Tea

Peppermint tea is traditionally enjoyed as a refreshing beverage. Many people enjoy it after meals or as part of their daily beverage routine.

2. Ginger Tea

Ginger tea is a warming beverage with a distinctive flavour. It can be enjoyed hot and is commonly prepared using fresh or dried ginger.

3. Chamomile Tea

Chamomile tea is often enjoyed as an evening drink and is commonly associated with relaxation routines.

4. Hibiscus Tea

Hibiscus tea is a colourful and refreshing drink that can be enjoyed either hot or cold.

5. Green Tea

Green tea is widely enjoyed around the world as an everyday beverage.

Important: Herbal products are not suitable for everyone. They may interact with medicines or may not be appropriate during pregnancy, for children, or for people with certain health conditions. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.
