Health & Medical Disclaimer

Important information about the use of our wellness content.

⚠️ Important Notice

The information provided by OLAMI HERBAL WELLNESS is intended for general educational and informational purposes only.

1. Not Medical Advice

The articles, guides, e-books and other information published on this website are not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as a personal recommendation to use a particular herb, supplement, product or wellness practice for the treatment of a medical condition.

2. Herbal Products Are Not Risk-Free

The fact that a product or ingredient is described as natural or herbal does not automatically mean that it is safe for everyone.

Some herbs and herbal products may cause side effects, allergic reactions or interactions with medicines.

3. Medicines and Health Conditions

If you take prescription or over-the-counter medicines, have an existing health condition, or have concerns about using an herbal product, speak with a qualified healthcare professional before using it.

4. Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

People who are pregnant, planning pregnancy or breastfeeding should seek appropriate professional medical advice before using herbal products or supplements.

5. Children and Older Adults

Herbal products may not be appropriate for children or older adults without professional guidance. Always consider individual circumstances before using any herbal product.

6. Product Information

Information about herbal products on this website is provided for educational purposes. Always read the product label, ingredients, directions and warnings provided by the manufacturer before use.

7. Individual Results May Vary

OLAMI HERBAL WELLNESS does not guarantee any particular health result from the use of herbal products, teas, supplements or wellness practices.

Individual experiences can vary depending on many factors, including personal circumstances, diet, medications and general health.

8. When to Seek Professional Help

If you experience serious, unusual or persistent symptoms, seek appropriate medical attention rather than relying solely on information provided on this website.

9. Educational Purpose

Our goal is to provide accessible educational information that helps readers understand herbal wellness and make more informed questions and decisions.

We encourage responsible use and consultation with qualified healthcare professionals whenever appropriate.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about our website, educational resources or digital products, please contact OLAMI HERBAL WELLNESS.
