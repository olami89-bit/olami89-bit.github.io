Herbal and natural products are not automatically risk-free. Some products may cause side effects, interact with medicines or may not be appropriate for certain individuals.

When to Seek Professional Advice

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before using herbal products for a health condition or when you have questions about whether a product is suitable for you.

Professional advice is particularly important when considering herbal products for children, during pregnancy or breastfeeding, when taking medicines, or when managing an existing health condition.
