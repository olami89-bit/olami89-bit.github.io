HERBAL TEA GUIDE

5 Popular Herbal Teas and What Beginners Should Know

A simple introduction to popular herbal teas and responsible everyday use.

Herbal teas are popular beverages made by infusing herbs, flowers, leaves, roots or other plant materials in hot water. They are enjoyed in many cultures and can be part of a person's everyday beverage choices.

However, herbal does not automatically mean risk-free. Different plants can have different properties, and some may not be appropriate for everyone.

For beginners, the best approach is to learn about the ingredients, preparation methods and relevant safety considerations before making herbal teas part of a regular routine.

1. Ginger Tea

Ginger is a widely used plant ingredient and is often prepared as a tea by steeping fresh ginger slices or using dried ginger.

Ginger tea is commonly enjoyed as a warm and flavourful beverage. Some people traditionally use ginger preparations as part of their general wellness routines.

When preparing ginger tea, use a reasonable amount of ginger and pay attention to how your body responds.

2. Hibiscus Tea

Hibiscus tea is commonly prepared from dried hibiscus calyces. It is known for its deep colour and naturally tart flavour.

It can be enjoyed hot or cold and is popular in many parts of the world.

People taking medication or managing a health condition should consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional before regularly consuming herbal preparations.

3. Peppermint Tea

Peppermint tea is prepared using peppermint leaves and is commonly enjoyed for its refreshing flavour and aroma.

It can be served on its own or combined with other herbs.

People who experience discomfort from peppermint should stop using it and seek appropriate professional guidance if necessary.

4. Chamomile Tea

Chamomile tea is traditionally prepared from chamomile flowers and is widely consumed as a herbal beverage.

It has a mild floral flavour and is commonly enjoyed as part of relaxing evening routines.

People with allergies, particularly sensitivities to plants in the same botanical family, should exercise appropriate caution.

5. Lemongrass Tea

Lemongrass is another popular ingredient used to prepare herbal tea. It has a distinctive citrus-like aroma and flavour.

Fresh or dried lemongrass may be used depending on the preparation.

As with other herbal products, individual responses can vary, so responsible use is important.

How to Prepare Herbal Tea

A simple herbal tea preparation generally involves placing the appropriate amount of plant material in hot water and allowing it to steep for a suitable period of time.

Preparation instructions can vary considerably depending on the ingredient and product. Always follow the directions provided on packaged products.

Important Things Beginners Should Know

  • Natural does not automatically mean safe for everyone.
  • Read product labels and preparation instructions carefully.
  • Check the ingredients before consuming a packaged herbal tea.
  • Individual responses to herbs can vary.
  • Some herbs may interact with medicines.
  • People with health conditions should seek appropriate professional advice.
