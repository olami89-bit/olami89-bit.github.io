7 Herbal Ingredients Beginners Should Know About

A simple introduction to popular herbal ingredients and responsible wellness education.

Herbs and plant-based ingredients have been used in different cultures for generations. Today, many people continue to explore herbs as part of their interest in natural living and wellness.

For beginners, however, it can be difficult to know where to start. Learning about common ingredients, their traditional uses, preparation methods and safety considerations can be a useful first step.

Here are seven popular herbal ingredients that beginners may come across.

1. Ginger

Ginger is a widely used plant known for its distinctive warm and spicy flavour. It is commonly used in cooking, beverages and herbal tea preparations.

Fresh ginger slices or dried ginger can be used to prepare tea. Ginger is also a common ingredient in many traditional food and beverage preparations.

When using ginger as part of your routine, follow appropriate preparation guidance and pay attention to your individual response.

2. Hibiscus

Hibiscus is widely used to prepare a colourful herbal beverage with a naturally tart flavour.

Dried hibiscus calyces can be infused in hot water and the resulting drink may be enjoyed hot or cold.

Hibiscus is particularly popular in many African communities and is often prepared as a refreshing beverage.

People taking medicines or managing a health condition should seek appropriate professional guidance before regularly consuming herbal preparations.

3. Peppermint

Peppermint is known for its refreshing aroma and cooling flavour. Its leaves are commonly used to prepare herbal tea.

Peppermint tea can be enjoyed on its own or combined with other herbs.

Individual responses can vary, so anyone who experiences discomfort after consuming peppermint should stop using it and seek appropriate advice if necessary.

4. Chamomile

Chamomile is a flowering plant commonly used to make herbal tea.

Its mild floral flavour makes it a popular choice for people looking for a gentle herbal beverage.

People with known plant allergies should exercise appropriate caution and consider professional advice when necessary.

5. Lemongrass

Lemongrass has a fresh citrus-like aroma and flavour and is commonly used in teas and cooking.

Fresh or dried lemongrass may be used depending on the preparation.

As with other herbal ingredients, responsible use is important and individual responses may vary.

6. Turmeric

Turmeric is a bright yellow-orange plant commonly used as a culinary spice.

It is also found in some herbal preparations and wellness products.

The amount and form of turmeric used can vary widely between food, beverages and supplements. Always read product information carefully and follow directions provided by the manufacturer.

7. Moringa

Moringa is a plant whose leaves are used in foods, powders, teas and other preparations in different communities.

Moringa products can vary considerably in form and concentration.

If using a packaged moringa product, check the label, ingredients, instructions and warnings before use.

Why Beginners Should Learn About Ingredients

Knowing the name of an herb is only the beginning. Different products can contain different forms, amounts and combinations of ingredients.

A responsible approach means looking beyond marketing claims and learning about what a product actually contains.

  • Check the ingredients list.
  • Read preparation and usage instructions.
  • Look for relevant warnings.
  • Avoid assuming that natural means risk-free.
  • Consider possible interactions with medicines.
  • Pay attention to how your body responds.
  • Seek professional advice when appropriate.
